Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about PUC certificates and our services

PUC (Pollution Under Control) certificate is a mandatory document that proves your vehicle meets the government's emission standards. It's required for vehicle registration renewal and to legally operate your vehicle on roads in India.

PUC certificates are valid for 6 months for 2-wheelers and 3-wheelers, and 12 months for 4-wheelers. You'll need to renew it before expiry to continue operating your vehicle legally.

You'll need your vehicle registration certificate (RC), driving license, and vehicle identification details (engine and chassis numbers). If renewing, any previous PUC certificate details are helpful.

Government fee for PUC testing is typically ₹100-₹150 depending on vehicle type. Ok Motors service fee is additional, but we ensure a hassle-free process including document verification and government submission.

Yes, vehicles of any age can get PUC as long as they're registered. However, older vehicles may need pre-testing modifications to meet emission standards. We can advise on this during the process.

Once you submit your details and payment, the testing and government submission typically takes 3-5 business days. You'll receive your digital certificate via WhatsApp or SMS as soon as it's issued by the government.

The government issues digital PUC certificates now. You'll receive it on WhatsApp or SMS, which you can display on your phone when needed. We also send you a copy for your records.

If your vehicle fails, you'll need to fix the emission-related issues (like spark plugs, filters, etc.). Once fixed, you can retake the test. We can guide you on likely issues and remedies.

For Patna and Muzaffarpur, we can arrange on-site testing in some cases. However, centralized testing ensures accurate results under controlled conditions. We handle all paperwork so you don't need to visit government offices.

Yes, you can renew PUC up to 30 days before it expires. This helps you avoid any legal issues from running an expired certificate. Contact us to schedule your renewal well in advance.
