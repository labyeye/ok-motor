Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about traffic challans and payment process

An e-challan is a digital traffic fine issued by traffic police for traffic violations like speeding, signal jumping, or wrong parking. It's recorded in the government system and must be paid to avoid legal consequences.

You can check pending challans using your vehicle's registration number (plate number) on the traffic police website or through Ok Motors. We can also verify for you and provide details of any outstanding fines.

Non-payment of challan can result in vehicle registration cancellation, driving license suspension, heavy penalties, or legal action. It's important to pay within the stipulated time to avoid these consequences.

Yes, you can file an appeal against a challan if you believe it was issued wrongly. You'll need to file an appeal through the traffic police website with supporting documents within 30 days of receiving the challan.

Typically, you have 30 days from the challan date to pay. Early payment may sometimes result in a discount. After 30 days, late fees and additional penalties may apply. We help you pay within the deadline.

Most RTO portals accept online payment via credit card, debit card, net banking, and UPI. Some also accept payments at designated traffic police offices. We assist with online payment which is the fastest method.

Yes, you receive a digital receipt immediately after successful payment which serves as proof of payment. We share this receipt with you via WhatsApp and maintain records for your reference.

Traffic tax is an annual/quarterly fee paid for vehicle registration and operation. Challan is a fine for traffic violations. We help with both tax payments and challan settlement for complete compliance.

Genuine challans are issued through official RTO systems and can be verified using your vehicle registration number. We verify challans through official channels to ensure authenticity before payment.

If you bought a vehicle with unpaid challans, the new owner is responsible for payment. We can help identify and settle any outstanding challans during RC transfer to avoid registration issues.
