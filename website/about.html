Founder & CEO

A Message From Our Founder

Welcome to Ok Motors, where passion for two-wheelers meets exceptional service. My journey began in 2010 with a simple love for motorcycles and a vision to create a trusted platform for bike enthusiasts.

What started as a small garage operation has now grown into one of the most reputable pre-owned bike dealerships in the region. Our success comes from treating every customer like family and every bike with the care it deserves.

At Ok Motors, we don't just sell bikes - we help you find the perfect ride that matches your personality and needs. Our team of experts ensures every bike in our inventory meets the highest standards of quality and performance.

Thank you for being part of our journey. We look forward to serving you and helping you find your dream bike.

Our Journey

1989

The Foundation

Ok Motors was established in Muzaffarpur, starting as a small two-wheeler repair and service center

1995

First Showroom

Opened our first dedicated showroom, marking the beginning of pre-owned bike sales

2005

Regional Expansion

Expanded operations across Bihar, establishing ourselves as a trusted name in pre-owned vehicles

2015

Premium Collection

Introduced our premium segment with high-end bikes and quality certified vehicles

2020

Digital Transformation

Launched our online platform and moved to our new 10,000 sq ft facility with state-of-the-art service center in Patna

2022

Multi-Brand Partnership

Established authorized partnerships with major brands and expanded our inventory to include premium cars alongside bikes

2024

Customer Excellence Milestone

Achieved 10,000+ satisfied customers and introduced comprehensive warranty programs and 24/7 customer support

2025

Innovation & Growth

Launched advanced vehicle valuation tools, introduced EMI financing solutions, and expanded to multiple service centers across Bihar
