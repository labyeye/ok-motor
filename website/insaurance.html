Insurance Assistance — How It Works

Contact & Request Submission

Call, WhatsApp or submit an enquiry form to request challan/insurance assistance.

Details Submission

Share vehicle details and any challan information so our team can begin verification.

Verification

We verify pending tax or challan details against the vehicle and confirm payable amount.

Insurance Request Created

We initiate the insurance/ challan payment request with the selected insurer on your behalf.

Payment Link Sent

The insurer sends a secure payment link to you; you can pay using available methods.

Receipt & Confirmation

After payment, the insurer shares the receipt and confirmation; we forward these to you via WhatsApp.
