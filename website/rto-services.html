Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about RTO services and vehicle documentation

RC transfer is the process of changing the vehicle ownership from the current owner to a new owner in the registration certificate. This is mandatory when buying or selling a vehicle and requires both parties' documentation and RTO approval.

RC transfer typically takes 15-30 days from the date of submission to the RTO, depending on the approval process and any additional document verification required by the authorities.

You'll need the original RC, valid ID proof, address proof, application form, NOC (if applicable), insurance certificate, pollution certificate, and for used vehicles, a sale/purchase agreement and affidavit.

HP (Hypothecation) termination is the process of removing the bank or finance company's claim on your vehicle. It's required when you've fully paid your vehicle loan and want to get the clear ownership certificate.

No, it's illegal to drive without a valid RC. During the RC transfer process, you'll need a temporary pass from the RTO. We handle all documentation to ensure you get this temporary authorization.

Re-registration is required when you change your address to a different RTO jurisdiction, shift to another state, or after major repairs/modifications. We assist with the complete re-registration process.

A fitness certificate proves that your vehicle meets safety and pollution standards set by the government. It's required for commercial vehicles and mandatory renewal for older vehicles to ensure roadworthiness.

NOC (No Objection Certificate) is required when transferring a vehicle to another state or if there are finance/legal claims on it. The bank or original owner issues it to confirm no pending obligations.

Government fees vary based on the service type. RC transfer costs ₹300-₹500, re-registration ₹500-₹1000. Ok Motors service fees cover documentation, verification, and RTO submission for hassle-free processing.

Yes, we handle inter-state transfers which require NOC from the original RTO, pollution certificate, and registration in the new state. We manage the entire multi-step process for you.
