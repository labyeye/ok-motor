Guarantee & Warranty

  1. Three-month Guarantee on Engine:
    The Seller provides a three-month guarantee on the overall ENGINE of the vehicle, starting from the date of delivery.
  2. Engine Warranty (6 months to 1 year):
    The Seller further provides a warranty on the engine for a period of 6 months to 1 year, starting from the date of delivery. This warranty covers any defects in the engine that may affect its performance or functionality.
  3. Exclusions:
    Please note that the guarantee does not cover the clutch plate. Any repairs or replacements required for the clutch plate shall be the sole responsibility of the Buyer.

Service Requirements & Notes

During the three-month guarantee period, it is mandatory for the Buyer to schedule and complete monthly servicing of the vehicle. Failure to do so may result in the expiration of the guarantee. However, the warranty (as mentioned in point 2) will still be available to the Buyer.

The initial three services come to you as a heartfelt gift, with no cost involved, apart from the nominal charge for engine oil if any changes are required. For any additional spare parts, excluding the engine itself, a reasonable fee will be applicable. In addition, we kindly request a modest 50% labor charge to be included, ensuring our dedicated team's exceptional service and attention to detail.

We kindly request your esteemed presence to provide us with the cherished photocopies of the sell letter and transfer challan, as they hold great significance in completing the process seamlessly. Your cooperation in this regard is deeply appreciated.

In the event of any potential defects discovered post-purchase of the vehicles, we humbly request you to promptly lodge a complaint via the registered mobile number within a generous timeframe of 24 hours. Failure to adhere to this guideline may regrettably result in the company incurring repair charges. Your timely communication is greatly valued, fostering a harmonious partnership.

Once the invoice billing is generated, we regretfully inform you that refunds will not be applicable. However, in the event of any challenges encountered during the transfer of vehicle ownership, we wholeheartedly commit to providing our esteemed customers with a complete refund only where explicitly warranted and after due verification.
